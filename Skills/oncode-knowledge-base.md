## 1. Company overview

### Mission & positioning

OnCode (also styled **On Code**, **Oncode**, **OnCode LLC**, **Oncode Software Solutions**, **OnCode Software Agency**) is an AI and custom software firm that finds where B2B/B2G operators leak time and money, then builds the sites, web apps, automation, and agentic workflows that seal those leaks — and manages what it ships after launch.

**Homepage headline (verbatim positioning):**  
> “On Code applies agentic AI and custom technology for B2B and B2G companies, so you waste less or produce more.”

They start with a **Bottleneck Discovery & Goal-Factoring Roadmap**, then deploy automated routines and agentic workflows the client’s team owns.

## 2. Services & offers

### Entry offer — Bottleneck Discovery / “first look”

Public offer on homepage:

- Map how the business actually runs; price every manual leak in hours and dollars; hand a ranked roadmap.
- **Investment model (site):** Flat fee agreed before start; if they move into a build, that fee **credits against the build** (not paying twice).
- **Time ask (FAQ):** About **three hours** total — one ~30-minute scoping call, short sits with people who do the work, roadmap walkthrough.
- Client owns the roadmap whether or not they build with OnCode.

### Four engagement tiers (“One ladder. Four rungs.”)

Every engagement lands on one tier; every tier sits on the same management foundation:

1. **Digital Presence** — Site, lead capture, channels customers already use.  
2. **Web Applications** — Booking, payments, client portals, admin dashboards.  
3. **Custom Software** — Replace spreadsheet/email workflows with process-owned software.  
4. **AI & Workflow Automation** — Automate intake, follow-up, invoicing, reporting, etc.

### Retainer — Ongoing Management

Hosting, monitoring, fixes, standing engineer on call. “Every system we ship lands on this layer.”

### Process (how they work)

**Diagnose → Specification → Development → Launch & Manage**

1. Bottleneck Discovery & Goal-Factoring Roadmap  
2. Spec aligned to business model (routines, agentic workflows, outcomes)  
3. Design & deploy; keep client updated  
4. Deploy, train team, move onto management layer  

**Ownership (FAQ):** Client owns roadmap, code, accounts, and data. Nothing held hostage.
